VTuber Project

Character Design · Motion · Streaming

I created a VTuber version of myself using VNyan/VNCTuber. The character's idle face was based on an image of myself generated with Stable Diffusion 1.5. I then generated additional facial expressions using ChatGPT.

I used Photoshop to create and refine the character's talking expressions and background.

Donation and Follow Animations

I created animated 2D pixel-art sprites for donation and follow notifications. I generated the initial pixel sprites using ChatGPT, then animated them in After Effects and cleaned them up in Photoshop.

Stream Scenes

I also created custom stream scenes that allowed me to quickly switch between different states during a livestream.
